# Human Docs

Write factual, plain documentation for the reader's next action.

## Workflow

1. Define the reader and their goal. Complete when the document's title and opening state the task in the reader's terms.
2. Gather evidence from source, configuration, or a repeatable command. Complete when every operational claim has a checkable basis.
3. Shape the path: prerequisites, action, expected result, and recovery where needed. Complete when the headings follow the reader's order of work.
4. Draft with concrete verbs, short sentences, and defined terms. Complete when examples can be used as written and placeholders are labeled.
5. Verify facts and remove stale detail. Complete when the document distinguishes facts, assumptions, and examples, and no claim exceeds its evidence.

Put warnings beside the action they affect. Link to authoritative material instead of duplicating it.
